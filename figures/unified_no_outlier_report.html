Interpretation

Search is stronger for: TwoImg>OneImg (Cut), JX>CA, JX>OI

Key insight: JX-related contrasts (JX>CA, JX>OI) and the Cut Effect (TwoImg>OneImg) are stronger during Search than at the Insight moment. This suggests:

  • Cut processing (integrating discontinuous images) happens during sustained search, not at the moment of insight
  • JX difficulty is reflected in effortful encoding/search processes
  • At the insight moment, CA shows stronger activation (reward/resolution), while JX effects diminish
  • The search model better captures haiku encoding processes that vary by haiku type
